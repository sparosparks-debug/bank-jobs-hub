Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice

How your recruitment credentials, contact information, and resume documents are safeguarded.

1. Purpose and Scope of Data Collection

The Govt & Private Bank Jobs Portal collects only personal and academic information strictly necessary to evaluate your eligibility for banking roles in accordance with public sector commissions and employer guidelines. This includes your name, email, mobile number, educational credentials, aggregate scores, and curriculum vitae (PDF format).

2. Password Encryption & Security Standards

All candidate and administrative passwords are irreversibly hashed using industry-standard cryptographic algorithms (PBKDF2 / SHA-256 / scrypt). Passwords are never stored in plain text. Session tokens are maintained via cryptographically signed HTTP cookies.

3. File Upload Safety & Restrictions

Resume uploads are restricted strictly to validated Portable Document Format (.pdf) files with an 8MB file size limit. Uploaded files undergo secure path sanitization to eliminate path-traversal vulnerabilities.

4. Access Control & Non-Disclosure

Candidate submissions are accessible exclusively to authenticated recruitment officers and board administrators. We do not sell, rent, or lease candidate data to external commercial advertising agencies.

For privacy inquiries or account data purge requests, contact our Compliance Officer at privacy@apexbankportal.gov.in.
